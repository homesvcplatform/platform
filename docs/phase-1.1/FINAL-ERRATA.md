## Part A: Architecture corrections (G-1…G-9)

| ID | Original problem | Approved resolution | Affected documents | Implementation impact | Applied |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **G-1** (X-09) | Bill creation was async after visit completion. The IVR/app couldn't reliably state the amount due, and cash recording could fail | **TCP-3:** `jobs.completeVisit` (and close-without-repair / cancellation-with-fee) → `payments.issueBill` in the same transaction. Bill = pure, deterministic function of the approved quote version + material usage + policy snapshot. **No ledger write** in that transaction (ledger posting is async in the worker) | 01 §5, §5.3 · 02 §3 · 09 §6 · README | Payments module exposes a pure `computeBill` + `issueBill` facade. The fitness-test TCP allowlist gets TCP-3. Completion endpoints return `amountDue` | Inline |
| **G-2** (X-08) | IVR offers could expire while the technician was pressing keys (double confirmation adds time) | Offer **hold** while an offer call is `IN_PROGRESS`: `held_until = call end + 30 s`, hard cap 6 min. The accept guard uses `max(expires_at, held_until)`. The sweeper treats held offers as live | 03 offers · 06 §5 · 07 §6.1 · 08 F1 | `matching.offers.held_until`. The voice module sets/clears the hold on call events. Tests for accept at second 299 of a held call | Inline (03, 07). Errata-only (06 §5 guard, 08 F1 note) |
| **G-3** (X-06) | "One pending offer per technician" blocked technicians holding long scheduled/direct offers from all other jobs | H10 applies only to offers with **overlapping windows**. ASAP offers: one at a time. Non-overlapping scheduled/direct offers may coexist. Enforced in the application under a per-technician advisory lock. Accept-time re-validation prevents conflicts | 03 offers · 07 H10, §6.1 | Matching candidate filter uses window overlap. Concurrency tests for mixed ASAP/scheduled offers | Inline |
| **G-4** (X-04) | L2 address disclosure didn't require the booking to be customer-verified (an ops-booked job could expose an address for an unverified, possibly fake booking) | L2 requires **window open ∧ (customer_verified ∨ an ops-confirmed call to the registered number, logged)**. Matching may proceed before verification. Disclosure waits | 02 §5, INV-17 · 06 §13 · 04 §10 | Disclosure service check + tests (time-travel × verification state) | Inline (02). Errata-only (04/06 wording) |
| **G-5** (X-10/11/12, SR-18) | Ledger write paths were unclear across roles. Grants contradicted call paths | **Only `app_worker` writes `ledger.*`.** API/admin/voice write business rows + outbox only. Grants: `app_api` INSERT `pricing.price_snapshots`. `app_voice` INSERT `payments.cash_collections`. `app_admin` no ledger writes | 03 §12.1 · 09 §6 · 01 §5 | Grant migration + a grant-matrix test that fails if any non-worker role can INSERT into `ledger.*` | Inline |
| **G-6** (X-13/15/16, SR-10/11) | The BFF had no way to validate sessions. The client-IP trust chain broke through the BFF. CORS was undefined | The BFF forwards `__Host-sid`, and **`api` validates sessions**. The BFF is stateless with no DB role. The BFF passes the client IP in a **signed internal header over mTLS**, which `api` trusts only from the BFF identity. `api`/`admin-api`/`hooks` send **no CORS headers** (deny by default) | 01 §2 · 04 §1.1 · 05 §2.1 · 13 ST-27 | BFF↔api mTLS, header signing key in Secrets Manager. Rate limiter reads the trusted header. Tests for spoofed headers and cross-origin preflight | Inline (01, 04). Errata-only (13 ST-27) |
| **G-7** (X-25, SR-07) | The design claimed erasure reaches backups "including" archives and providers, which overstated the guarantee | See SR-07 below: a column→key-subject mapping, pseudonymous-only archives, minimised and short-lived provider payloads, provider deletion requests, honest DSR statements, and erasure re-applied after restores | 03 §14.3/§14.8 · 10 §5/§7 · 14 §5 · Phase 0 §11.6 | Crypto library needs a key-subject registry. Archive jobs drop PII columns. DSR template text | Inline (03 §14.8). Errata-only (others) |
| **G-8** (X-21/22) | Visit-fee credit was modelled differently in quotes vs bills. A prior paid visit-fee bill couldn't be credited | Quote items gain `VISIT_FEE_CREDIT`. Bill lines gain `PRIOR_PAYMENT_CREDIT` with a required `prior_bill_id`. Amounts stay ≥ 0, and credit types are subtracted. Version/bill totals = Σ lines (with credits negative in the sum). Ledger: the credit transfers from the settled receivable of the prior bill | 03 quote_items · 09 bill_lines, §4 | Pricing engine emits credit lines. Property tests: totals = Σ signed lines. A prior credit never exceeds the prior settled amount | Inline (DDL). Errata-only (09 §4 posting text) |
| **G-9** (X-17, X-18) | One support agent could capture a diagnosis and record the customer's approval of it. Ops-recorded approval was a routine channel | Separation of duties: **recorder ≠ verifier ≠ diagnosis capturer** (DB CHECK + policy). Ops-recorded approval is an **accessibility fallback, disabled by default**, ≤ ₹1,000 provisional, SMS-code read-back identity check, recording subject to D-15. Also applies to other approvals: maker ≠ checker (INV-19), appeal reviewer ≠ original reviewer, payout batch approver ≠ preparer, assist-mode grantor ≠ the agent | 02 INV-08 · 03 quote_approvals · 05 §5.3 · 04 §22 | Policy checks + DB CHECKs + matrix tests. Feature flag `ops_recorded_approval=false` | Inline (02, 03). Errata-only (05/04 role text) |

## Part C: Security specification fixes

SR-01 (telephony webhook authenticity) remains a **hard blocker for production IVR state changes**. Its spike protocol is in [phase-2/03 §Spike S-1](../phase-2/03-phase-2-gates.md#spike-s-1-telephony-authenticity-sr-01). None of the items below claims zero risk.

| ID | Updated specification | Automated / operational test | Residual risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SR-02** Field-agent browser sessions | Agent web uses BFF cookie sessions (`__Host-sid`, HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax), server-side session, CSRF synchronizer token, strict CSP + Trusted Types, 12 h absolute / 30 min idle. No bearer tokens or PII in browser storage | E2E asserts no tokens in `localStorage`/`sessionStorage`/IndexedDB. Cookie-flag test. CSRF negative tests. CSP violation reports at 0 in E2E | **Medium:** agents use unmanaged devices. Malware on the device can still act within an active session |
| **SR-03** Assist-mode passkey step-up | An assist grant is issued by ops for (visit, agent) ≤ 2 h. Activation requires a **WebAuthn (passkey) assertion** bound to the grant. TOTP-only agents can't activate. The technician is notified by SMS. Grants are rate-limited per agent (e.g., ≤ 5/day). Grantor ≠ agent | Activation fails without a passkey. Expiry enforced. Notification sent. A grant for an unlinked technician is rejected. Rate limit | **Low–Medium:** a compromised device with an unlocked passkey can still misuse a live grant (bounded by scope and time) |
| **SR-05** Signed-link hardening | Token in the **URL fragment** only, POSTed in the body. 256-bit, single-version, expires with the version, single successful decision. `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`. Generic page for link-preview user agents. OTP always goes to the registered number | Log canary: no tokens in CDN/WAF/ALB/app logs after link E2E. Replayed decision → 409. Preview-bot UA gets generic content | **Low:** a shared-phone browser history may still hold the fragment. Possession alone can't approve without the OTP |
| **SR-06** KMS/PII blast radius | Separate CMKs per data class (`pii-contact`, `pii-address`, `kyc`, `recordings`, `restricted-attributes`). Per-role key policies (e.g., `voice`: contact+address only. `api`: contact+address. Admin reveal path via `admin-api`: per-class). **Encryption context** `{subject_id, data_class}` on every wrap/unwrap. Decryption only via the disclosure/reveal services (no generic helper exported). DEK cache ≤ 5 min. **CloudTrail→EventBridge decrypt-rate alarms per role** | Role without class grant → decrypt fails (integration test against LocalStack/KMS mock + staging check). Alarm fires on a simulated bulk decrypt in staging. Lint: no import of the raw crypto helper outside allowed modules | **Medium:** an attacker inside a role can decrypt at the allowed rate until detected. Detection + revocation is the control |
| **SR-07** Erasure, backups, providers | (1) A **key-subject registry**: every encrypted column declares its subject (customer, technician, or "multi-party → platform key + minimisation"). (2) Erasure = null PII + destroy the subject key + delete files + **provider deletion requests** (SMS/WhatsApp BSP/telephony/AI/KYC) via vendor APIs or tickets, tracked to completion. (3) Archives hold pseudonymous data only. (4) Provider event payloads minimised and kept ≤ 180 d. (5) Backups: PITR 35 d + monthly snapshots (retention ⚖️). Platform-key-encrypted residual PII in backups is **acknowledged in the DSR response** with its expiry date. (6) The erasure ledger is re-applied after any restore | Erasure E2E: no decryptable PII in DB/S3/AI logs. Sampled archive check. Provider-deletion tracker completes. Restore drill re-applies erasures. DSR response template includes residuals | **Medium:** data inside third-party systems and backups depends on vendor compliance and backup expiry. ⚖️ legal acceptance of the backup approach needed |
| **SR-08** PDF/HTML rendering | Invoices/evidence packs are generated with a **programmatic PDF library (no HTML/JS engine)** and bundled fonts. If HTML rendering is ever required: a separate task with **no network egress, no IMDS**, read-only FS, escaped inputs, a timeout | Injection payloads (HTML/JS/URLs) in names/addresses render inert. Network-isolation test for any renderer task | **Low:** PDF library vulnerabilities (patch cadence) |
| **SR-10** Client-IP trust chain | CDN → BFF: trust `X-Forwarded-For` only from CDN prefix lists. BFF → api: signed `X-Client-IP` header (HMAC, key in Secrets Manager) over mTLS. api accepts it only from the BFF's mTLS identity. Otherwise it uses the socket peer | Spoofed headers from non-BFF sources ignored. Rate-limit tests keyed correctly | **Low:** IPs are weak identifiers anyway (CGNAT on Indian mobile networks). Limits also key on phone/device/session |
| **SR-11** CORS deny-by-default | No CORS headers on api/admin-api/hooks. Preflights → 403 | Cross-origin fetch/preflight tests | **Low** |
| **SR-12** Provider-side DTMF logging | Provider selection requires gather-input masking or no logging of DTMF/recognised digits (spike S-1 checks this). Provider call-log retention minimised. Console access SSO+MFA with named users. PIN rotation forced if a provider breach is notified. Codes are single-use per visit | Spike checklist item. Quarterly provider console access review (operational) | **Medium:** provider staff/systems may still see digits transiently. PINs protect medium-value actions only |
| **SR-13** Code coercion/forwarding | Customer copy: share the start code only at the door with the person whose photo matches, and the completion code only when satisfied. After completion: "Was the work completed? 1 yes / 2 no" (IVR/PWA) with a 24 h dispute window. Adult-present policy. Completion code entered **> N min before** expected work duration → flagged | Copy reviewed in usability tests. Post-completion confirmation sent (E2E). Early-entry flag test | **Medium:** social coercion can't be eliminated technically |
| **SR-14** SIM swap → customer approvals | Approvals above the threshold need step-up. **Approvals from a device first seen < 24 h ago above the threshold → held for a confirmation call to the registered number** (or a WhatsApp confirmation if the WhatsApp of record is unchanged). Approval receipts to the registered number + WhatsApp of record | New-device high-value approval is held. Receipt sent | **Low–Medium:** a full SIM + WhatsApp takeover can still pass. Damage is limited by the amount and the dispute window |
| **SR-15** AI/audio leakage | AI features off by default (flags). Text suggestions only with redaction (names/phones/addresses tokenised). **Audio transcription only** with (a) India-region or self-hosted processing, (b) a DPA with no retention/training, (c) customer consent for transcription. Otherwise human listening only. AI outputs are closed enums, with no side effects. Prompts versioned | Redaction unit tests with Telugu/English PII samples. Flag-off → no provider calls (egress test). Consent gate test | **Low** while off. **Medium** when enabled (vendor dependency) |
| **SR-16** CI/CD image signatures | The pipeline verifies cosign signatures + SBOM before deploy. **Only the pipeline deploy role** can register task definitions/update services (SCP + IAM). Images by digest. An EventBridge/Config rule alerts on any task definition with an unknown or unsigned digest. Branch protection + 2 reviewers on CI config | Attempted manual task-definition registration by a non-pipeline role → denied (staging). Unsigned image → pipeline fails | **Low–Medium:** compromise of the CI system itself (mitigated by OIDC scoping and protected environments) |
| **SR-17** SOS independence from the admin IdP | SOS alerting doesn't depend on the console or IdP: telephony ring group + SMS to on-duty safety staff + **provider static fallback** + a second messaging provider. Degraded-mode runbook ([SAFETY-OPERATIONS](SAFETY-OPERATIONS.md)) | Monthly drill with the IdP and console disabled. Synthetic SOS checks. Provider failover test | **Medium:** total telephony + mobile network outage in the area. Mitigation is the 112 guidance |
| **SR-18** Ledger-write restriction | See G-5 | Grant-matrix test | **Low** |
| **SR-19** Manual-pilot PII sprawl | Company phones/WhatsApp Business only. One access-controlled workspace with a restricted PII tab. Addresses by voice only. No customer numbers shared with technicians. Weekly access review. Purge within 30 days of pilot end unless the customer consents to migration. Disposition logged ([09 §7](09-manual-pilot.md)) | Operational: weekly access-review checklist signed. End-of-pilot purge certificate. Spot audits of technician WhatsApp for leaked addresses (with consent) | **Medium-High:** manual processes leak. The pilot is time-boxed |
| **SR-20** Shared devices | ≤ 2 technician accounts per device, enforced. App lock mandatory when > 1 account. Local data partitioned per account and purged on account switch. Flag for review | Device-binding tests. Purge on switch. Third account rejected | **Low–Medium:** family members with the device PIN |
| **SR-21** IVR approval caller identity | ID check ("Am I speaking with {first name}?"). Amount readback. Limit ₹2,000 **provisional** (D-12, prototype + legal dependent). Approval receipt SMS. 24 h dispute window. Above the limit → link + OTP | Simulator tests. Customer IVR mini-test (1.1/07 §9) must pass before enablement | **Medium:** another adult in the household can answer and approve |
| **SR-23** Vendor due diligence | A mandatory checklist before any contract: India data location, DPDP processor terms, sub-processors, breach notice ≤ 24 h, deletion SLA + API, SSO+MFA consoles, audit reports (ISO 27001/SOC 2) or questionnaire, **webhook signing**, DTMF/log retention controls, pen-test summary, exit/data-return plan. Reviewed annually | Operational: signed checklist per vendor stored in the vendor register. Gate blocks production credentials without it | **Medium:** vendor assurances aren't independently verifiable |
